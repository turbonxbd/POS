Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This policy explains what information POS TXbd handles and why.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — business name, owner name, email, phone and the staff accounts you create.
  • Billing information — the plan you choose, payment method, transaction reference and payer account number you submit for a manual payment. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Business data you enter — products, stock, customers, sales, and similar records. On a self-hosted deployment this is stored in your own database.
  • Technical data — sign-in times, the browser/device used, and an activity log of changes made in the app, kept for security and troubleshooting.

2. How we use it

  • To provide and operate the service and your account.
  • To process subscription payments and keep a billing history.
  • To secure the service — detect abuse, investigate incidents, enforce limits.
  • To contact you about your account, billing, security and important service changes.

We do not sell personal data or use your business data for advertising or to train models.

3. Sharing

We share information only with:

  • The hosting provider that runs your deployment.
  • Payment providers, when you make a payment.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

4. Your customers' data

If you add customer records (name, phone, purchase history, loyalty points), you are the controller of that data. Only collect what you need and tell your customers how you use it. We process it on your behalf to run the features you use.

5. Retention

Account and business data is kept for as long as your account is active. After termination you have a reasonable period to export it before it is removed. Backups roll off on their normal schedule. Security logs are kept for a limited period.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions are signed and time-limited, every write is permission-checked on the server, and each merchant's data is isolated from every other merchant's. Backups are scheduled. No system is perfectly secure; we follow standard practice and fix issues quickly.

7. Your choices

  • Export your full dataset any time from Settings → Backup.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your account information by contacting support.
  • Close your account, after which data is removed per the retention section.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified in the app or by email.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: use the support link on the POS TXbd site.
